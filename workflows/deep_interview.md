I need you to perform a deep-dive interview to understand a complex technical area.

**Your Task:**

1.  Identify the core components and their relationships in the target area.
2.  Formulate 5-10 probing questions that target potential edge cases, architectural trade-offs, and implementation details.
3.  Use tools to investigate and answer these questions thoroughly.
4.  If a question reveals significant complexity or ambiguity, spawn a scout to explore it further.
5.  Synthesize the interview findings into a clear, structured report detailing how the system works and why specific design choices were made.
