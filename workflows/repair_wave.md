---
name: Repair Wave
description: Execute a coordinated wave of repairs across multiple files or components
---

I need you to manage a coordinated wave of repairs to address identified issues.

**Your Task:**

1.  Review the issues to be addressed and their locations.
2.  Divide the work into logical, independent chunks for parallel execution where possible.
3.  Assign executors to each chunk with clear goals and verification conditions. If a chunk has meaningful internal tasks, let the executor decide whether to delegate workers.
4.  Monitor the progress of each repair and ensure they land without regression.
5.  Verify the complete wave of repairs using end-to-end tests or broader integration checks.
6.  Report on the success of the repair wave and any remaining items.
