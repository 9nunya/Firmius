---
name: Evidence Sweep
description: Systematically gather evidence to verify a claim or investigate a bug
---

I need you to perform a systematic sweep to gather evidence for a specific investigation.

**Your Task:**

1.  Define the scope of the sweep and what constitutes valid evidence.
2.  Use `grep`, `glob`, and `list_directory` to find relevant code snippets, logs, and configuration files.
3.  Execute tests or diagnostic commands to capture runtime behavior.
4.  Catalog each piece of evidence with its source and what it proves or disproves.
5.  Organize the findings into an evidence ledger that can be used for debugging or verification.
