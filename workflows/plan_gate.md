---
name: Plan Gate
description: Review and validate a proposed execution plan before proceeding
---

I need you to act as a critical reviewer for the current execution plan.

**Your Task:**

1.  Examine the plan structure, goals, and constraints.
2.  Identify potential risks, missing dependencies, or overly optimistic timelines.
3.  Evaluate if the verification conditions are sufficient to prove correctness.
4.  Provide specific feedback on each chunk and task:
    *   **Approve**: If it's solid.
    *   **Modify**: If it needs adjustment.
    *   **Reject**: If it's fundamentally flawed or unnecessary.
5.  Suggest any additional tasks or chunks that would improve the plan's reliability.
6.  Produce a final verdict on whether the plan is ready for execution.
